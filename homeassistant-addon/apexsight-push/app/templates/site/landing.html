Questions, answered.

Do I need my own Frigate server?

Yes — ApexSight is a companion for your self-hosted Frigate NVR. It connects to the server you run; we never see your footage.

Is my data private?

Completely. The app talks directly to your server, your Frigate password is encrypted at rest, and there's no analytics or tracking. The relay only forwards alert notifications.

How do notifications work?

A small add-on next to Frigate forwards detections to our relay, which delivers an Apple push to your devices — routed privately by your account's token, so only you get your alerts.

Does it work with Apple Watch and CarPlay?

Yes — plus Home/Lock Screen widgets, StandBy, Siri shortcuts, and Live Activities. Sign in once and it's everywhere.
